The teams that reach Level 3 will win this market.

Level 1: AI assists typing (autocomplete, suggestions)
Level 2: AI assists thinking (chat, planning, code review)
Level 3: AI executes supervised work (agents code, test, deploy — humans approve)
The gap between Level 1 and Level 3 is not incremental — it’s 20x-50x throughput
The limiting factor is not the model. It’s how you use it.

Agentic Maturity Ladder

Most teams are currently somewhere between Level 0 and Level 1
Level 0

Waterfall / Agile

Humans execute delivery with minimal or ad-hoc AI use across the SDLC.

Level 1

AI assisted

Humans execute delivery and apply AI to individual tasks within the SDLC.

Examples
Autocomplete, explanations, debugging help, drafting tests.

Level 2

AI directed

Humans set intent and specifications, directing AI to execute phases and prompt for validation.

Examples
Clarifies intent, creates specs, generates architecture, code, and tests.

Work unit
Features

SDLC process
SDLC becomes a faster guided pipeline with AI.

Level 3

AI delegated

AI runs asynchronously as a system of agents end-to-end; humans review exceptions and approve.

Examples
Agents triage, replicate, build, test, and return a proposed solution.

Work unit
Full build

SDLC process
SDLC becomes an asynchronous agent loop.

Timing
Hours, asynchronous

Value
% of tickets deflected away from developers

Impact
20x potential ballpark improvement

Manual human intervention AI autonomy
The operating model shifts from humans doing the work to humans directing and approving the work.
