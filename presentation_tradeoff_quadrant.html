Presentation Design Tradeoffs

Memorable, accurate, simple, complete.

Every presentation is a balancing act: make it easy to understand, precise enough to trust, memorable enough to stick, and complete enough to act on.

Memorable sticky, emotional, story-led
Information-Dense complete, detailed, comprehensive

TED Talk Clear, simple, and unforgettable

The best deck is not the one with the most information.

It is the one that gives the audience enough clarity to understand, enough accuracy to trust, enough detail to act, and enough story to remember.

1
TED TalkHigh memory, high accessibility — but often simplified.
2
Expert KeynoteMemorable and credible — but can become too expert-heavy.
3
Executive BriefClear and useful — but may not be emotionally sticky.
4
Technical DocumentationAccurate and complete — but rarely remembered as a story.
Design goal: move toward the sweet spot without pretending one slide can be a speech, a briefing note, and a technical manual all at once.
